The seven rounds, 255 screens built from their maps with their enemies and bosses, Rastan in his three layers, the dragon stage the game skips and an ending it never draws, all drawn from the ROM.

Taito - (c) Taito 1988 - A 256 KB MegaROM (ASCII8) - MSX2 - Dump 03fa40fa...

Not one capture here. Every picture is drawn from the bytes of the ROM: the title, the 257 screens of the 24 stages, Rastan, the 37 actor types and the bosses, with the cartridge's own tables, and checked against openMSX on a Japanese MSX2: the title and 256 screens without a single difference; the figures, sprite record by sprite record. The listing and the numbers are reproducible with make, and reassembling gives back the ROM byte for byte.
